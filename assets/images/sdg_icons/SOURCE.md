# SDG goal icons

The 17 official UN Sustainable Development Goal icons, one set per app
language, resized to 360x360 and saved as WebP (quality 90).

| Folder | Source |
|---|---|
| `en/` | `https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/goals/E_SDG_Icons-NN.jpg` |
| `ja/` | `https://www.unic.or.jp/files/sdg_icon_NN_ja_2.png` (UNIC Tokyo) |
| `es/` | `S-WEB-Goal-NN.png` from `https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/10/S-SDG-Icons-2019-WEB.zip` |

The EN JPEGs carry a 1px pale compression seam on their edges, trimmed
before resizing. JA goals 13 and 15 had transparent edge rows, filled with
the goal colour.

Use follows the UN SDG guidelines for informational, non-commercial use:
`https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/news/communications-material/`
(Japanese edition: `https://www.unic.or.jp/files/SDG_Guidelines_AUG_2019_Final_ja.pdf`).
Apart from the edge fixes above the icons are unaltered and shown square, as
the guidelines require; the About screen credits the United Nations.
